Air dispersion screening

Screening-level dispersion modelling for dust and emissions around industrial, mining and construction sites — cross-referenced with drone-captured aerial imagery to identify sources, receptors and wind-driven plume paths.

Approach

Screening before detailed modelling

Before commissioning full regulatory dispersion models (e.g. AERMOD, CALPUFF), a screening assessment estimates likely plume extent and identifies sensitive receptors — helping prioritise where detailed monitoring or modelling is actually needed.

Reading the plume

Concentration decreases with distance

Dust and emission concentrations fall off rapidly with distance from the source — following an approximate Gaussian plume relationship dependent on wind speed, atmospheric stability and source emission rate.

We model this against your site layout, identifying the distance at which concentrations are predicted to fall below guideline thresholds, and highlighting any sensitive receptors (residences, schools, water bodies) within that footprint.
