Instrument validation

Reliability & validity testing

Before drawing conclusions from a survey instrument, we test its internal consistency. Cronbach's alpha above .70 is generally considered acceptable, above .80 good, and above .90 excellent — values approaching 1.0 can indicate redundant items.

Item-total correlations identify any survey items that don't cohere with the rest of the scale, so they can be reviewed or dropped before the main analysis runs.
