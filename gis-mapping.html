Reading the index

NDVI & NDRE explained

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) compares red and near-infrared reflectance: healthy, dense vegetation absorbs red light and reflects NIR strongly, producing values toward 0.9. Bare soil, water and stressed or senescent vegetation produce values near zero or negative.

NDRE substitutes the red-edge band for red, making it sensitive to chlorophyll and nitrogen changes at the canopy level even before visible discolouration — useful for early intervention in irrigation, fertilization or pest management.

NDVI = (NIR−Red) / (NIR+Red) NDRE = (NIR−RedEdge) / (NIR+RedEdge)

Why overlap matters

High forward and side overlap ensures every ground point appears in multiple images from different angles — the redundancy photogrammetry software needs to triangulate accurate elevation and eliminate parallax distortion in the final orthomosaic.

Ground control & RTK

The onboard RTK module receives correction signals to tag each image with survey-grade coordinates in real time, removing the need for manually placed ground control points in most surveys — though we deploy GCPs for projects requiring independent third-party verification.
