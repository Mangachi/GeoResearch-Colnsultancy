Day/night comparison

Why night-time matters most

Regulatory noise limits are typically stricter at night, reflecting increased sensitivity to disturbance during sleeping hours. Monitoring at multiple boundary points allows us to identify which receptors are closest to or exceeding applicable limits.

Where exceedances are identified, we model the contribution of individual noise sources (e.g. specific equipment, traffic) to prioritise mitigation measures.

Octave band frequency profile
