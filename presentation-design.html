Structure

A narrative arc, not a slide dump

Most defence decks fail not because the research is weak, but because the slides bury the argument in detail. We structure each deck around a small number of key messages, with supporting detail held in appendix slides for Q&A.

Typical structure: context and problem (2–3 slides), objectives and methodology (3–4 slides), key findings (4–6 slides, one message per slide), discussion and contribution (2–3 slides), and a closing summary slide.
