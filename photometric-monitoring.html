Illumination & light-level surveys

Photometric monitoring

Light-level surveys for workplace, construction and environmental compliance — measured against recognised lux standards and mapped across your site to identify under- and over-lit zones.

Methodology

Grid-based illuminance surveys

Using calibrated lux meters, we record illuminance at a regular grid of measurement points across the site, capturing both horizontal (workplane) and vertical (task-area) illuminance where relevant.

Reference standards

Benchmarked against recognised guidance

Measured values are compared against task-area illuminance recommendations — for example general office work (~300–500 lx), fine assembly or inspection tasks (~750–1000 lx), and warehouse circulation areas (~100–150 lx).

Where measured levels fall outside the recommended range, we flag the zones on a site plan and provide remediation recommendations — fixture relocation, additional luminaires, or maintenance of existing fittings.

Applications

Where photometric surveys are used

Workplace safety compliance

Verifying that work areas meet occupational health and safety lighting requirements.

Construction site lighting

Assessing temporary lighting adequacy for night works and pedestrian safety on active sites.

Environmental impact assessment

Measuring light spill and sky-glow contribution near sensitive habitats as part of EIA studies.

Retail & commercial fit-out

Verifying merchandising and display lighting meets design intent after installation.

Energy audits

Identifying over-lit areas as part of broader energy efficiency studies.

Agricultural & greenhouse studies

Light intensity mapping for crop trials investigating photosynthetically active radiation.
