Study design & sampling

Methodology design

Sound methodology is the backbone of credible research. We design study approaches, sampling strategies and instruments that hold up to committee and reviewer scrutiny — matched to your questions, population and resources.

Approach

Choosing the right design

The right design depends on your research question, not the other way around. We help you choose — and justify — an approach that fits what you're trying to find out.

Quantitative — survey & experimental

Cross-sectional surveys, quasi-experimental and experimental designs for hypothesis testing and generalizable findings.

Qualitative — case study & ethnographic

In-depth interviews, focus groups, case studies and thematic analysis frameworks for exploratory questions.

Mixed methods

Sequential or concurrent designs combining quantitative breadth with qualitative depth.

Sampling strategy

Probability and non-probability sampling techniques, with sample size justification and power calculations.

Instrument development

Questionnaires, interview guides and observation checklists — piloted and refined for validity.

Ethical clearance support

Informed consent forms, ethics application narratives and institutional review board (IRB) submission support.

Getting the numbers right

Sample size & power

An underpowered study risks missing real effects; an oversized sample wastes time and resources. We calculate the minimum sample size needed to detect the effect size you care about, at your chosen significance level and statistical power.

For qualitative designs, we apply saturation-based reasoning and document the rationale for your sample size in a way reviewers expect to see.
