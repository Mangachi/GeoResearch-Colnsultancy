Research proposal writing

Concept notes through full proposals — structured to funder and institutional requirements, with a clear problem statement, justified methodology and realistic budget narrative.

Scope

From a one-page concept to a full submission

We work with academic researchers, NGOs and small enterprises preparing proposals for university review boards, grant funders or institutional donors. Every proposal is structured to the specific guidelines of the target institution.

Concept notes

A tight 1–3 page summary of the problem, objectives and approach — used to test funder interest before a full proposal.

Problem statement & justification

Framing the research gap with supporting literature and a clear statement of significance.

Objectives & research questions

SMART objectives and research questions or hypotheses aligned to your methodology.

Literature review framing

Positioning your study within existing scholarship and identifying the gap it addresses.

Budget narrative

Line-item budgets with justification text matched to funder templates and ceilings.

Work plan & timeline

Gantt-style activity schedules showing milestones, deliverables and reporting points.

How it runs

A structured drafting process

We typically work in weekly cycles: an initial scoping call to understand your research idea and target funder, followed by drafted sections delivered for your review and feedback.

Each draft comes with tracked changes and comments explaining the reasoning behind structural choices — so you understand not just what was written, but why.
